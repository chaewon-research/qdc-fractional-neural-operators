## Interpretation

- **FULL END-TO-END** means the archive contains the training/evaluation path needed to generate fresh outputs from the declared configuration.
- **FULL SELECTED-RANK RERUN** means the paper's frozen selected rank is evaluated end-to-end, while the historical calibration search is documented separately.
- **FULL VALIDATION-SELECTION WORKFLOW** means all declared candidate ranks are swept on disjoint validation artifacts, the frozen selection rule is applied, and only the selected rank is subsequently evaluated on the held-out artifacts. The multi-gigabyte prediction artifacts are generated by the high-resolution training workflow and are not bundled in this compact archive; use `RUN_TRAIN=1` on a fresh checkout.
- **VERIFIED HISTORICAL SELECTION RECORD** means the rule and selected result are preserved, but unavailable historical calibration rows are not fabricated.
- **HISTORICAL MEASUREMENT + REFERENCE COMPONENTS** is used for the specialized A100 path because the archive includes the timing protocol and low-level CUTLASS component but does not claim that those files alone reconstruct the original integrated benchmark executable.
